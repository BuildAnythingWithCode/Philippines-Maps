Welcome To Philippines Maps!

This website was born out of frustration of lack of a clear, centralized Philippines public transportation system. It pulls the Leaflet JavaScript API with added-in functionality to allow commuters to contribute to a shared userbase of public transpo route data.

How It Works

  1. Create an account (to cut down on spam, and to make sure you're human 🤖)
  2. Submit your own knowledge of jeepney, trycicle, and bus routes local to your area 🚌
  3. Upvote and downvote other users' route submissions to help keep Philippines Maps accurate ✅

Philippines Maps will be free and open-sourced, forever and always. See the source code here.
