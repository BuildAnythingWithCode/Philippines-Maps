Welcome To Philippines Maps!

This website was born out of frustration with lack of a clear, centralized Philippines public transpo system. It pulls maps API's with built-in functionality to allow Filipino users to contribute to a shared user-base of public transpo route data.

How It Works

  1. Create an account (to cut down on spam, and to make sure you're human 🤖)
  2. Submit your own knowledge of jeepney, trycicle, and bus routes to help others get around 🚌
  3. Upvote and downvote other users' route submissions, to help keep Philippines Maps accurate & up-to-date ✅

Philippines Maps will be free and open-sourced, forever and always. See the source code here.
